## Demo recording script (60–90 s, sample chat only)

| Time | Show |
|---|---|
| 0–10 s | The problem: a long group chat, "who said they'd do what?" |
| 10–25 s | Landing → **Try with a sample chat** → parse summary (people, messages, dates) |
| 25–45 s | Review: open a task's source message, tap ✓ and ✗, watch precision update, enter missed count |
| 45–75 s | Dashboard: tick a task (strike-through + **Undo**), confirm a "Suggested done", People tab, Budget totals |
| 75–90 s | "Parsed on your device; AI only with your consent." + the link |

On Windows: Win + G (Xbox Game Bar) records the browser window. On a phone, use the built-in screen recorder.
